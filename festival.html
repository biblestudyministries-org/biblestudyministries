Annual Gospel Festival

Bringing Christians together for worship, evangelism, fellowship and celebration of the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

About the Festival

The Annual Gospel Festival is one of BSM's flagship events, uniting churches and believers from across denominations for a time of praise, worship, preaching and fellowship.

Proposed Activities Include:

  • Gospel music performances by local and international artists
  • Preaching and biblical teaching from noted ministers
  • Interdenominational worship services
  • Prayer sessions and testimonies
  • Youth and children's activities
  • Christian leadership discussions and workshops
  • Evangelistic outreach and altar calls
  • Exhibitions by Christian organizations

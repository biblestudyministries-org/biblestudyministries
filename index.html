Our Mission

Proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ through the study of His Word, worship, and prayer.

BSM fulfils its mission by providing accessible Bible study programmes, organizing worship and prayer gatherings, conducting evangelistic outreach, developing Christian leaders, and using media and publications to share biblical teachings.

Our Vision

Winning Souls to Jesus Christ.

BSM envisions a world in which people encounter Jesus Christ, grow in their faith, and become active participants in sharing the Gospel and serving their communities.

Core Christian Values

  • FaithHebrews 11:1Trusting God and believing in His promises, even during difficult times.
  • LoveMatthew 22:37–39Loving God wholeheartedly and loving others as ourselves.
  • IntegrityProverbs 10:9Being honest, truthful, transparent and upright in words and actions.
  • HumilityPhilippians 2:3–4Putting God first, serving others, remaining willing to learn and grow.
  • ServiceMark 10:45Using our gifts, time and resources to serve others and glorify God.

Who We Serve

  1. Individuals exploring Christianity for the first time
  2. Christians seeking spiritual growth and deeper Bible understanding
  3. Members of different Christian denominations
  4. Pastors, church leaders and ministry workers
  5. Young people and emerging Christian leaders
  6. Children and families in early childhood development
  7. Gospel musicians, worship teams and content creators
  8. Churches seeking training and strategic planning
  9. International Christian ministries and partners

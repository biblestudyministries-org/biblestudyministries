How to Participate

  1. Register using the contact form to receive your Bible study calendar
  2. Set aside time daily for personal reading and reflection
  3. Join the live session weekly or watch the recording
  4. Engage with study notes and discussion questions
  5. Share testimonies and pray with other participants

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior Bible study experience?

No. The programme is designed for believers at all stages, from those new to the Christian faith to mature Christians seeking deeper understanding.

Can I participate from outside Malawi?

Yes. The programme is accessible worldwide through our online platforms.

Is there a fee to join?

The basic Bible study programme is free. Some specialized training courses may have associated costs or sponsorship opportunities.

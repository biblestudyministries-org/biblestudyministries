Our Projects

Early Childhood Development

Supporting the spiritual, educational, social and moral development of young children through age-appropriate Christian learning, family support and community-based initiatives.

Target: Children aged 0-12 and their families

Relief and Education

Supporting children from disadvantaged backgrounds, developing early childhood Christian learning initiatives, providing educational materials and organizing community learning activities.

Focus on vulnerable families and communities

Medical Care and Community Health

Organizing health awareness campaigns, facilitating medical outreach with qualified providers, supporting vulnerable patients and promoting preventive health education.

Partnering with hospitals, clinics and health organizations

Evangelism Outreach

Community evangelism, street outreach, hospital and prison ministry, and follow-up discipleship programmes.

Regular Saturday outreach and special campaigns

Community Support

Various community development initiatives including skills training, micro-enterprise support and humanitarian assistance.

International Christian Exchanges

Promoting learning, collaboration and fellowship between BSM and Christian organizations in other countries through exchange visits, joint prayer meetings and shared resources.

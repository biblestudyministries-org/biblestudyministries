About Us

Organizational Background

Bible Study Ministries (BSM) is a Christian ministry committed to winning souls to Jesus Christ through the proclamation of the Gospel, Bible study, prayer, praise and worship, evangelism, leadership development, and Christian fellowship. The ministry seeks to unite Christians across different denominations, provide opportunities for spiritual growth, equip Christian leaders, and reach individuals who have not yet accepted Jesus Christ as their Lord and Saviour.

Vision

Winning Souls to Jesus Christ.

BSM envisions a world in which people encounter Jesus Christ, grow in their faith, and become active participants in sharing the Gospel and serving their communities.

Mission

Proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ through the study of His Word, worship, and prayer.

The ministry fulfils its mission by providing accessible Bible study programmes, organizing worship and prayer gatherings, conducting evangelistic outreach, developing Christian leaders, and using media and publications to share biblical teachings.

  • FaithHebrews 11:1Trusting God and believing in His promises, even during difficult times.
  • LoveMatthew 22:37–39Loving God wholeheartedly and loving others as ourselves.
  • IntegrityProverbs 10:9Being honest, truthful, transparent and upright in words and actions.
  • HumilityPhilippians 2:3–4Putting God first, serving others, remaining willing to learn and grow.
  • ServiceMark 10:45Using our gifts, time and resources to serve others and glorify God.

Ministry Focus

Interdenominational Bible Study

Bringing Christians from different churches together to study Scripture and promote unity.

Christian Leadership Training

Equipping pastors, church leaders and ministry workers with biblical knowledge and practical tools.

Early Childhood Development

Supporting spiritual, educational, social and moral development of young children.

Annual Gospel Festival

A yearly Christian gathering featuring praise, worship, preaching and fellowship.

Strategic Areas

  1. Spiritual Growth Through Fellowship, Praise and Worship – Regular worship sessions, online prayer requests, testimonies and livestreams.
  2. Bible Study and Prayer – One-Year Bible Study programme, study guides, daily reflections and prayer meetings.
  3. Winning Souls Through Outreach – Community evangelism, street evangelism, door-to-door outreach and follow-up discipleship.
  4. Relief and Education – Supporting disadvantaged children, educational materials and community learning activities.
  5. Christian Publications and Distribution – Bible study manuals, devotionals, sermon notes and digital resources.
  6. Medical Care and Community Health – Health awareness campaigns, medical outreach and preventive health education.

Organizational Leadership

BSM establishes clear responsibilities for programme management through proposed functional areas:

  1. Ministry Leadership and Governance
  2. Bible Study and Discipleship
  3. Praise and Worship
  4. Outreach and Evangelism
  5. Christian Education and Leadership Training
  6. Media, Communications and Publications
  7. Early Childhood Development
  8. Relief, Medical Care and Community Support
  9. Annual Gospel Festival
  10. Partnerships, Resource Mobilization and International Exchanges
  11. Finance, Administration and Accountability

These are proposed functional areas rather than separate departments. Roles may initially be combined depending on resources and staffing.

Statement of Faith

BSM affirms the historic Christian faith as expressed in the Apostles' Creed and Nicene Creed, including the inspiration of Scripture, the Trinity, salvation through Jesus Christ, and the expected return of Christ.
